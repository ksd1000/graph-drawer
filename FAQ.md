# FAQ

## Why does not expr-core.c use tokenization and AST when parsing expressions?

The author is not familiar with tokenization and AST(may be the content of *the theory about compiler*), thus can only use a hard way in place. That is, to scan character-by-character and divide them into some unary and binary operations.

## Will it use tokenization and AST in parsing expressions in the future?

Maybe it will be refactored in the future, but the date will be not assured and not optimistic(the author has not studied the relevant theory and has no time to study it at the moment), depending on when the author has the ability to implement it.
