# FAQ

## Why does libexpr not use tokenization and AST when parsing expressions?

The author is not familar with tokenization and AST, thus can only use a hard way in place. That is, to scan character-by-character and divide them into some unary and binary operations.

## Will it use tokenization and AST in parsing expressions in the future?

Maybe it will be refactored in the future, but the date will be not assured, depended on when the author be with the ability to implement it.
